Ghost Hunterz Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This policy describes the current ad-free version of Ghost Hunterz, an Android game published under the developer name Kaztech Games. It covers the game and privacy inquiries sent to us.

Ghost Hunterz runs offline. The game does not send your personal information or gameplay activity to us. It has no player accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, or embedded analytics services.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best scores, selected difficulty, music and sound preferences, and character unlock status in its private app storage. These records allow the game to remember your settings and achievements between sessions. We do not receive these records through the game, sell them, or share them with advertisers.

Android backup and platform services

Android backup is enabled for the game. Depending on your device and account settings, Android or your device provider may back up and restore saved game data, including during a device transfer or reinstallation. We do not receive or control those backups. You can manage backup settings through your device or account.

Google Play, Android, and your device provider operate their own services under their own privacy policies. Their handling of downloads, platform diagnostics, and backups is separate from the game's local storage.

Permissions

Ghost Hunterz uses the vibration permission for gameplay feedback. The current game does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions.

Children's privacy

The same offline data practices apply to children and adults. The game does not ask children to provide their name, email address, location, or other personal information, and contains no chat or social-sharing features. Parents or guardians may contact us about privacy questions. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact us on their behalf.

When you contact us

If you email games@kaztech99.com, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your inquiry and provide support. Our email service provider processes the message to deliver and store it. Please do not send passwords, identity documents, or unnecessary personal information.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to address the inquiry and any related support or legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing the same address. Any information that must be retained to meet legal obligations will be limited to that purpose.

Deleting your data

You can remove local scores, preferences, and unlocks using Android Settings: open Apps, choose Ghost Hunterz, then choose Storage and Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names vary by device. Uninstalling the game also removes its local app data. This resets saved progress.

Device backups are managed separately and may restore previous saves when you reinstall. Use your device or backup provider's settings to manage those copies. There is no Ghost Hunterz player account to delete and no developer-hosted gameplay profile.
