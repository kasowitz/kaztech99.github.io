Delete Your Ghost Hunterz Data

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Ghost Hunterz has no player accounts. The only data we keep on our server is the optional leaderboard profile, and you can delete it yourself in the game at any time.

Delete your leaderboard data in the game

If you joined the leaderboard, this removes your Ghost Tag, ghost picture and all your leaderboard scores from our server right away:

  1. Open Ghost Hunterz and tap Leaderboard on the main menu.
  2. Tap Edit Ghost Tag.
  3. Tap Remove Me, then confirm with Remove me.

You need an internet connection for this step. If it can't reach our server, the game says so and nothing changes, so you can try again later. Your scores stay saved on your device, and you can join again later with a new Ghost Tag.

Do this before you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Uninstalling or clearing storage does not remove your leaderboard data from our server, and afterward the game can no longer find it. If that has already happened, email us (see below).

Delete your leaderboard data by email

Email games@kaztech99.com with the subject "Ghost Hunterz leaderboard deletion". Different players can choose the same Ghost Tag, so please include:

  • your Ghost Tag (for example "Spooky Owl 42") and which ghost picture you chose;
  • one or two boards you played and your approximate scores (for example "Moonveil Manor, Easy, about 12,000").

We will delete the matching leaderboard profile and its scores, and reply once it's done. Children should ask a parent or guardian to email us.

What gets deleted

  • Deleted from our server: the random ID the game created for the leaderboard, your Ghost Tag, your ghost picture, and every leaderboard score. Nothing from your profile stays on the live leaderboard. Copies may remain in occasional server backups for up to 30 days before those backups are replaced.
  • Kept on your device: your scores, unlocks, spin tokens, settings and saved age group. They were never on our server. To delete them, use the steps below.

Delete the game data on your device

Everything else Ghost Hunterz saves stays on your device. To delete it, uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Ghost Hunterz → Storage → Clear storage. This removes your progress, unlocks, settings and saved age group. If Android backup is turned on, your device or Google account may also have a backup copy, which you can manage in your device's backup settings.

Ad data

Optional rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob. We do not receive or store Google's ad data, so we can't delete it. You can review Google's controls in its Privacy Policy. In the game, use Ad Privacy on the Unlock Wheel to review your ad privacy choices.
