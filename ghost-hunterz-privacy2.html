Ghost Hunterz Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This policy covers Ghost Hunterz by Kaztech Games. Version 0.8.0 remains ad-free and works offline. Versions 0.9.2 and 0.9.3 include optional Google AdMob test rewarded ads. Test ads do not generate advertising revenue. Subsequent live-ad versions will use the privacy flow described below.

Saved game data

Scores, settings, favorite characters, unlocks and spin tokens are saved in the game's private storage on your device. The game has no player account or developer-hosted gameplay profile. Kaztech Games does not receive your saved progress through the game.

Optional rewarded ads

In ad-enabled versions, you can choose to watch an ad to earn a spin token. Ordinary gameplay does not require watching ads. Ads require an internet connection. Google Mobile Ads may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, ad interactions, diagnostic data, and device or app identifiers to deliver and measure ads and help prevent fraud. Google processes this information under its Privacy Policy. Removing the Android advertising ID permission does not prevent all other SDK data processing.

The app requests non-personalized ads and limits requested ad content to Google's G rating. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. The app removes Android advertising ID and Android ad-services permissions. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location access. It uses network permissions for ads and vibration for feedback.

Age and privacy choices

Before requesting an ad, the app asks for your age. It saves only whether you are under 18, not your exact age. Players under 18 receive child-directed and under-age-of-consent ad settings. Unknown ages cannot request an ad until this step is completed. You may cancel and continue playing without an ad.

Live-ad versions use Google's User Messaging Platform to check applicable privacy choices before requesting ads. Use Ad Privacy on the Unlock Wheel to access available privacy options. The current test versions use demo ads and does not display live consent messages. Parents and guardians may contact us about children's privacy. There is no in-game chat.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Clearing storage also removes progress and the saved age group. Android backup is enabled: device or account settings may back up and restore this data. Manage those copies with your device or backup provider. We do not control Google's ad-data retention; refer to Google's privacy policy and privacy controls for its practices.

Contacting us

Email games@kaztech99.com for privacy questions. We receive your address and message and use them to provide support. Our email provider processes correspondence. We retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations; you can request deletion at the same address. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact us.
